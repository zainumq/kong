How it works

  1. Mint — buy Mint Passes with USDT (1 pass = 1 share, max 10 per wallet). Earlier tiers are cheaper and claim more $KONG.
  2. Graduation — when all 10,000 shares sell, 250M $KONG + 80% of the USDT open a PancakeSwap pool (liquidity locked forever), 20% goes to the treasury, and claims open.
  3. Claim — burn your pass for its $KONG (60,000 / 50,000 / 40,000 by tier). Passes are tradable on secondary markets before that.
  4. Refund — if the sale does not sell out in 30 days, a 14-day refund window opens: burn your pass for a 100% USDT refund.
  5. Refer — share your link and earn 3% of your referrals' claimable $KONG, claimable after graduation.
  • Total supply: 1,000,000,000 $KONG — 485M sale claims, 250M liquidity, 100M treasury (vested), 100M team (vested), 15M referral pool, 50M community.
  • Opening pool price at graduation: 250M $KONG + 12,600 USDT ≈ $0.0000504 per $KONG (at full sell-out).
  • Admin: none. No owner, no pause, no upgrade, no withdraw function. The curve, the tiers and the refund rules are immutable.
  • Pass royalty: 5% to the treasury on secondary sales (ERC-2981).
